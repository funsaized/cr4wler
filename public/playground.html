Somewhere between the hyperlinks and the footnotes, there is a small, opinionated arachnid. It has eight exceptionally long legs, an eye for interesting typography, and absolutely nowhere to be.

This is Cr4wler. A little guest in your browser. It will inspect a phrase, find a good handhold, and gently rearrange your reality. Nothing important goes missing. Reset brings every word home.
